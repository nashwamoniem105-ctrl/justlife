Gather Your Cleaning Supplies First

Before you begin, make sure your cleaning kit is ready to go. While deep cleaning doesn’t require overly complicated products, using effective and eco-friendly solutions can make the job easier and safer.

Recommended cleaning supplies:

  • Protective gloves – To shield your skin from harsh cleaning agents.

  • All-purpose cleaner – Store-bought or homemade.

  • Glass cleaner (Windex) – For windows, mirrors, and shiny surfaces.

  • Bicarbonate of soda (baking soda) – Excellent for scrubbing and deodorizing.

  • White vinegar – A natural disinfectant and limescale remover.

  • Dish detergent – Cuts grease on surfaces and cookware.

  • Sponges – Use separate ones for kitchen and bathroom areas.

  • Microfiber cloths or paper towels – For streak-free drying.

  • Vacuum with attachments – To reach into corners and crevices.

  • Mop or steam cleaner – For floors.

  • Hard-floor broom or vacuum – For sweeping before mopping.

Tip: Keep all supplies in a portable cleaning caddy so you can easily move from room to room without forgetting anything.

Bathroom Deep Cleaning

  1. Tub, fixtures, and grout

    • Use a baking soda and water paste for stubborn stains.

    • Scrub fixtures with a toothbrush to reach small gaps.

    • Polish chrome taps with a microfiber cloth for shine.

  2. Shower curtain & liner

    • Machine wash on a gentle cycle if fabric or plastic is washable.

    • Air dry completely to prevent mold growth.

    • Replace liners that are worn out or discolored.

  3. Mirrors & tiles

    • Wipe with vinegar and water solution for streak-free results.

    • Pay attention to corners and edges where soap scum collects.

Kitchen Deep Cleaning

  1. Fridge & freezer

    • Unplug and remove all food.

    • Wash shelves and drawers with warm soapy water.

    • Vacuum the coils and clean under the fridge if possible.

  2. Cabinets & drawers

    • Empty and wipe down inside with a damp cloth.

    • Use a crevice attachment to vacuum crumbs from corners.

  3. Tables, chairs, and furniture

    • Wipe all surfaces, paying attention to chair legs and undersides.

  4. Garbage bins

    • Scrub with hot soapy water and disinfect to remove odors.

Bedroom Deep Cleaning

  • Wash duvet covers, pillow shams, and bed skirts according to care labels.

  • Clean under and behind furniture, especially the headboard.

  • Dust all surfaces with a damp microfiber cloth.

  • Rotate or flip mattresses every six months for even wear.

Living Room & Common Areas

  • Vacuum upholstery, including under seat cushions.

  • Wipe wooden or metal furniture with a damp microfiber cloth.

  • Clean carpets and rugs—consider steam cleaning for a deeper refresh.

Closets & Storage Areas

  • Remove all items, sort, and declutter.

  • Wipe shelves, walls, and baseboards before replacing items.

  • Avoid overcrowding clothes to allow air circulation.

General Home-Wide Cleaning

  1. Baseboards & walls

    • Wipe down with a non-abrasive sponge to remove scuff marks and dust.

  2. Light fixtures & ceiling fans

    • Turn off power before cleaning.

    • Use a damp microfiber cloth to remove dust.

  3. Floors

    • Sweep or vacuum, then mop thoroughly.

    • Spot-clean stains on carpets with a vinegar-water-dish soap mix (test first).

  4. Curtains & blinds

    • Wash or dry clean curtains if fabric allows.

    • For blinds, use a dampened sock over your hand to wipe slats individually.

  5. Windows & sills

    • Remove dust before using glass cleaner.

    • Use a squeegee for streak-free results.
