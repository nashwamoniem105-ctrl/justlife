Home cleaning may be a daunting task for many of us. House cleaning, if done sloppy, can matter next to nothing like your home will get messy sooner than expected. In this article, we’ll provide you with in-depth information about how to clean a house professionally and how to clean a home, fast, just like Justlife does it!

 

1. Change Your Soap

Before even beginning to answer your burning question of “How to clean home?” or “How to clean home fast?”, we need to go back to the basics. Before you even pick up a scrub brush, you can start cleaning smarter. Soaps made with glycerin or vegetable oil will keep your shower walls and floor looking cleaner for longer. Traditional soaps made from tallow can leave white, sticky soap scum on your shower walls. 

 

2. Put Your Phone Down

Even professionals get distracted sometimes. Even at home, you get distracted. Simplify your routine by getting rid of as many interruptions as you can: Set your phone to vibrate, turn off your computer and TV, and put off detailed projects like cleaning out the linen closet or putting away winter hats and scarves until later.

 

3. Start With the Easy Stuff

Start with something easy. Clean up a few rooms that don't get much use quickly and easily. This will get you motivated to clean more difficult areas like the kitchen and bathrooms. You'll feel like finishing the rest of your house. Depending on your family, the dining room, living room, or home office are good places to start.

 

4. Work With the Dishwasher

Is your toothbrush holder a little dirty? How about that drain for the dish? The dishwasher cleans soap dishes, toothbrush holders, dish drains, range hood filters, and more without any trouble. If it's made of hard plastic, glass, ceramic, or metal and fits, you can use it.

 

5. Develop a Squeegee Habit

Who here likes to clean the bathtub and shower? The easiest way to do less of this annoying task is to buy a squeegee and tell everyone in the house to use it after taking a shower or bath. Wipe down the walls, the sides of the tub, and even the floor (send the water straight toward the drain). Soap and shampoo residue won't evaporate and leave a film on your porcelain or stone because the water will wash it away. If you do this every day, you'll only have to clean the tub and shower once a month.

 

6. Steam Your Microwave

You might have taken a break from cleaning to make some tea. You might always check the microwave when you clean the kitchen. Either way, now that you've opened the door, you can't hide the food splatters on the walls, ceiling, and floor of this appliance. Don't worry, just try this: Fill a mug or measuring cup with water and heat it in the microwave for 2 to 3 minutes until it's nice and steamy. The steam will soften the dirt and make it easier to wipe away.

 

7. Give Your Vacuum a Tune-up

Use a canister, upright, bagless, or bagged vacuum, depending on your preference and the type of floor you have, but make sure to keep it in good shape for the best suction and efficiency. Every month, change the disposable bags, wipe the inside and bottom of the machine, and clean the rotating brush.
